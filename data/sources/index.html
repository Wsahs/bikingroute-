Review summary and limitations

Latest database expansion

285,149 address records are indexed locally: 145,785 in the Boca area and 139,364 in the Hollywood area. Source manifests record every downloaded ID inventory and checksum. Address coverage is not guaranteed complete.

The GIS evidence index contains 173,087 records. Boca routing now covers west to longitude -80.245 and contains 34,299 OSM highway ways plus 3,176 imported existing county pedestrian features. Source-coordinate connections are preserved; missing joins are not invented.

Not route-ready: overlapping sources need reconciliation. Proposed facilities, missing geometry, bicycle permissions, crossings, bridges and disconnected endpoints require review. Road centerlines are not sidewalk geometry. Unmapped does not mean nonexistent.

Miami-Dade download is blocked by certificate validation; no records are claimed for it. Broward coverage is limited to the Fort Lauderdale pilot.

Each dataset includes source metadata, retrieval date and a checksum. Source record counts can overlap; they are not unique streets or miles.
